# Pricing and getting into the market: notes to revisit

A first proposal, written 22 September 2026 and not yet decided. Nothing here is built. The prices are judgement, not research: check them against current Nigerian subscription prices and competitors before choosing.

## Principles

- **Free covers the daily habit, forever.** The plan, safe to spend, manual and bank-alert entries and goals must never sit behind a paywall, or nobody forms the habit and nobody tells friends.
- **Charge for what costs us money per person.** Live bank sync (Mono charges per linked account) and Flux (AI costs per question). That's easy to explain and protects our costs.

## Plans to test

| Plan | Price to test | What's in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | ₦0 | Full personal plan, safe to spend, manual and bank-alert entries, goals, one shared budget, a small monthly allowance of Flux questions |
| Plus (personal) | about ₦1,500 a month, or ₦12,000 a year | Live bank sync, more Flux, household budgets and helpers, exports, extra Wrapped |
| Business | about ₦5,000 to ₦7,500 a month | Unlimited invoices, team members, payroll and payslips, statement imports, tax filings |
| Business free tier | ₦0 | About 5 invoices a month, no team, so owners can try it |

- Annual plans with two months free. Many people prefer paying once, and it cuts churn.
- Paystack with bank transfer and USSD, not cards only.
- App stores take 15 to 30% of purchases made in the app. Check their current rules on selling outside the app before relying on web checkout.

## Referral reward

Referrals are already recorded (see FUNCTIONAL.md, "Invite friends"), so a reward can be given for past referrals too.

- Before launch: earlier access (the waitlist already moves people up 10 places per friend).
- Once Plus exists, proposed: one free month of Plus for both people when the friend counts, capped at about 12 a year.

## Getting into the market

1. **Start with one group.** Salaried people aged 22 to 35 in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt whose "salary finishes before the 9th". Second: small business owners who mix personal and business money. Having both in one app is what sets us apart.
2. **Launch in stages.** Waitlist, then a closed beta of 200 to 500 people (top referrers first), then a public launch that is free only for about 3 months. Switch Plus on once people keep coming back, for example 25% or more still active after 30 days.
3. **Cheap, trusted channels first.** WhatsApp sharing through referral codes, personal finance creators on Instagram and TikTok, posts timed to payday (25th to 30th), NYSC corps members, campus groups and churches. Paid Instagram ads alongside (waitlist/instagram/ads/AD-GUIDE.md).
4. **Use employers.** A business that runs payroll in the app can invite its staff to the free personal plan.
5. **Lead with trust.** "Read-only, we never move your money" everywhere. Register with the NDPC (Nigeria's data protection regulator) and publish a privacy policy, which Meta also requires for lead ads.
