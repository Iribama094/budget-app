Refund policy

How refunds will work for BudgetFriendly. Last updated 24 September 2026.

Right now

BudgetFriendly is free while we are getting started. Joining the waitlist is free, and the current app does not charge you, hold your money, or move your money. Because there is no payment today, there is nothing to refund today.

If we introduce paid features

Before anything is charged, we will show the price, billing period, what is included, renewal terms, cancellation steps, and this refund policy at the point of purchase. Paid features will always be optional.

  • If you are charged by mistake, we will refund the mistaken charge.
  • If a paid feature you bought is not provided, we will provide it, correct it, replace it, or refund you.
  • If a store or payment provider processes the payment, their refund flow may also apply.
  • Nothing in this policy limits any right or remedy you have under Nigerian consumer law.

How to ask

Email support@budgetfriendly.ng with “Refund” in the subject line and include the email address on your account, the date of the charge, the amount, and what went wrong.

Business details

BudgetFriendly is operated from Nigeria. The registered business name, CAC number, physical address, and paid-service contracting entity must be published here before paid subscriptions or purchases launch.
