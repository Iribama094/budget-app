Is it safe?

Your money never moves through us.

BudgetFriendly records and explains your money. It never holds it, sends it or spends it.

GTGTBank ACAccess ZEZenith UBUBA FBFirst Bank KUKuda OPOpay MOMoniepoint and 20 more

Read-only access

We use Mono, a licensed open-banking provider, to read your account history. Nothing more.

We never see your password

You sign in on your bank’s own page, not ours.

No transfers, no debits, ever

We can’t move money, and you can disconnect your bank at any time.

Your data stays yours

Flux answers from a summary of your own numbers. Your data isn’t used to train anyone’s AI model.

Questions

Before you join

When does BudgetFriendly launch?

We’re putting the finishing touches on it now. Everyone on the waitlist hears first, and early joiners get in first.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining the waitlist is free, and we’ll share pricing before launch.

Does it work with my bank?

It connects through Mono to GTBank, Access, Zenith, UBA, First Bank, Kuda, Opay, Moniepoint and about 20 more. You can also log spending by hand, by voice, or by pasting a bank alert.

Can BudgetFriendly move my money?

No. Bank access is read-only. We can’t make transfers or debits, and you can disconnect at any time.

My income isn’t steady. Will it still work?

Yes. Your plan uses a safe estimate of what you earn, and you update it whenever you get paid.

I run a business. Is this for me?

Yes. Your Business space keeps profit, invoices, uploads, payroll and tax apart from your personal budget, in the same app.
