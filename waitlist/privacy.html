Privacy policy

How BudgetFriendly handles your information. Last updated 22 September 2026.

BudgetFriendly is a money app for people and small businesses in Nigeria. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else sees it and what you can ask us to do. We are the data controller for this information.

The short version

  • We record and explain your money. We never hold it, send it or take it.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • Bank connections are read-only, and we never see your bank password or PIN.
  • You can export everything you have entered, or ask us to delete your account.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist

  • Your first name and email address.
  • Your WhatsApp number, only if you give it.
  • What you want to use the app for, and any money problems you tick.
  • Whether you asked for launch news, and the invite or campaign link that brought you.

When you use the app

  • Your account: name, email address and password. Passwords are stored by our sign-in provider as a one-way hash, so nobody at BudgetFriendly can read yours.
  • Your money records: income, spending, budgets, goals, bills, debts, what you own, and for businesses, invoices, customers, suppliers, staff pay and statements.
  • Bank information, if you choose to link a bank through our partner Mono: your account details and transactions, read-only.
  • Your device: the phone name and type, so you can recognise your own devices and sign out ones you do not, and a notification token if you allow notifications.
  • What you ask Flux, our money coach, so it can answer.
  • Voice notes you record to log spending. They are turned into text and never stored by us.

We do not use advertising trackers, and this website sets no cookies.

Why we use it

  • To give you the service you asked for: your plan, your figures, your reminders and your tax estimates. Our basis for this is performing our agreement with you.
  • To keep your account safe: telling you about new sign-ins and password changes, and spotting misuse. Our basis is our legitimate interest in security, and our legal duties.
  • To send launch news and money tips, only if you asked for them. Our basis is your consent, and every message has an unsubscribe link.
  • To make the app better, using totals and patterns rather than singling anybody out.

Who else sees it

Only the companies that help us run the service, and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase: hosting, the database and sign-in. Your data is stored in London, United Kingdom.
  • Brevo: sending emails such as codes, reminders and news.
  • Mono: bank connections, if you link a bank.
  • Groq: the AI behind Flux and voice entry. What you ask, and a voice note you record, are sent there to be answered or turned into text. We do not keep either on our side afterwards; their own policy governs what they do with it.
  • Expo: delivering notifications to your phone.
  • Vercel: hosting this website.

We may also share information if the law requires it. If BudgetFriendly is ever sold or merged, your information may pass to the new owner, who would be bound by this policy.

Because these companies are outside Nigeria, your information is transferred abroad. We rely on their contractual commitments to protect it to the standard Nigerian law requires.

Staff access

Our own team can see how your account is set up, for example whether you finished setting up your plan and how many devices you use, so they can help when you write in. They cannot see your transactions, and there is no way for anybody here to sign in as you. Every staff action is recorded in a log that cannot be edited or deleted.

How we protect it

  • Everything travels over an encrypted connection, and the database is encrypted where it is stored.
  • Your data is separated per account at the database level, so one account cannot reach another's.
  • Sign-in on your phone is kept in the phone's secure keystore, and you can lock the app with Face ID or a fingerprint.
  • Staff need a password and a code from an authenticator app.
  • We will never call, text or message you asking for your password or a code.

How long we keep it

  • Your money records, for as long as your account is open.
  • Waitlist details, until we launch and for a reasonable period afterwards, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Security records such as sign-in alerts and staff action logs, for as long as we need them to keep accounts safe.
  • If you delete your account, we remove your personal information, keeping only what the law requires us to keep.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your information, or send it somewhere else;
  • correct anything wrong;
  • delete your account and your information;
  • stop using your information in a particular way, or withdraw consent for news and tips;
  • explain any decision we have made about you.

In the app you can export your data at any time from Settings, and unsubscribe from any email we send. For anything else, write to us at the address below and we will answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

BudgetFriendly is not for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app or by email when the change matters.

Contact us

Email support@budgetfriendly.ng with "Privacy" in the subject line, and we will come back to you.
